I am a computer scientist working on how large, sharded databases move into cloud analytics storage. The question I keep returning to is practical: how to make that move faster, cheaper, and ready for reporting and for model training.

I completed an M.Sc. in Computer Science at the University of Lethbridge in 2024. Dr. Wendy Osborn supervised my thesis on batch migration from Apache ShardingSphere with PostgreSQL into AWS object storage in Delta Lake format. That work was accepted to IEEE COMPSAC 2025, and I presented it in Toronto.

I work remotely as a permanent database administrator and report developer at Sofvie. I started there as a student database administrator in February 2024 and became full-time on September 3, 2024. I am also a Mitacs Business Strategy Intern at the University of Lethbridge, developing a product for Trueffect in partnership with the university, and a co-founder of Huroca. The work is in cloud data management, including Apache Spark, Snowflake, AWS, and dbt.
