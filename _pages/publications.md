## Peer-reviewed

**Naveen Kumar Vadlamudi** and Wendy Osborn. 2025. Batch processing algorithms for NewSQL-based big data systems. In *Proceedings of the 49th IEEE Annual Computers, Software, and Applications Conference (COMPSAC)*, pages 301–306, Toronto, Canada, 8–11 July 2025. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/COMPSAC65507.2025.00048) · [IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11126763)

Algorithms for moving a sharded NewSQL database into cloud object storage. The paper compares sequential and concurrent Spark strategies and is aimed at cutting migration time, cloud cost, and the wait before data can be used for reports and model training. I presented this work in the Data Science and Data Engineering track.

Sidney J. Shapiro, Vivian Oystrick, Alison Liu, and **Naveen Kumar Vadlamudi**. 2024. From spreadsheets to scalability: Databases as a next step for non-profits. *Canadian Journal of Program Evaluation*, 39(2), 363–372. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3138/cjpe-2023-0050)

When program-evaluation data outgrows spreadsheets, and how a non-profit can choose among SQL, NoSQL, and desktop databases without ignoring staff, training, and infrastructure limits.

Vadlamudi Naveen Kumar and N. Ravi Shankar. 2020. Cryptanalysis of a new cryptosystem of color image using a dynamic-chaos Hill cipher algorithm: A chosen ciphertext attack. In *Progress in Computing, Analytics and Networking: Proceedings of ICCAN 2019*. Springer. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-981-15-2414-1_47)

A chosen-ciphertext break of a published color-image cipher. The scheme's chaotic key schedule reduces to an affine Hill cipher, and the attack recovers the confidential keys.

## Thesis

**Naveen Kumar Vadlamudi**. 2024. *Cost-effective batch-based migration strategies for NewSQL-based big data systems*. M.Sc. thesis, Department of Mathematics and Computer Science, University of Lethbridge. Supervisor: Wendy Osborn. [Handle](https://hdl.handle.net/10133/6939) · [OPUS record](https://opus.uleth.ca/items/0ef314c7-5dc8-4cb5-98a3-81ff6156201c)

Batch loads, in Apache Spark, from Apache ShardingSphere with PostgreSQL into AWS object storage as Delta Lake. The thesis compares shard-by-shard and aggregated-shard algorithms, each run sequentially and concurrently, and places the result in a lakehouse layout for analytics and machine learning.

**Naveen Kumar Vadlamudi**. 2023. Query processing in multi-cloud systems. Course report, CPSC 5990, University of Lethbridge. Instructor: Wendy Osborn. [GitHub](https://github.com/VadlamudiNaveen/Query-Processing-in-Multi-Cloud-Systems)

A course report on OLAP and OLTP systems and how queries are processed across cloud databases.
