publications

2025

  1. COMPSAC
    Batch Processing Algorithms for NewSQL-based Big Data Systems
    Naveen Kumar Vadlamudi and Wendy Osborn
    In Proceedings of the 49th IEEE Annual Computers, Software, and Applications Conference (COMPSAC), 2025
    Abs DOI Bib HTML

    Compares Apache Spark batch strategies for moving a sharded NewSQL database into cloud object storage. The aim is a shorter migration, a lower cloud bill, and data that is ready sooner for reports and for training models. Presented in the Data Science and Data Engineering track, Toronto, 8–11 July 2025.

2024

  1. M.Sc.
    Cost-effective batch-based migration strategies for NewSQL-based big data systems
    Naveen Kumar Vadlamudi
    University of Lethbridge, 2024
    Abs Bib HTML

    Supervised by Wendy Osborn. Implements batch loads in Apache Spark from Apache ShardingSphere with PostgreSQL into AWS object storage as Delta Lake, and compares shard-by-shard and aggregated-shard algorithms run sequentially and concurrently.

  2. CJPE
    From Spreadsheets to Scalability: Databases as a Next Step for Non-Profits
    Sidney J. Shapiro, Vivian Oystrick, Alison Liu, and Naveen Kumar Vadlamudi
    Canadian Journal of Program Evaluation, 2024
    Abs DOI Bib

    When program-evaluation data outgrows spreadsheets, and how a non-profit can choose among SQL, NoSQL, and desktop databases while accounting for staff, training, and infrastructure.

2020

  1. ICCAN
    Cryptanalysis of a New Cryptosystem of Color Image Using a Dynamic-Chaos Hill Cipher Algorithm: A Chosen Ciphertext Attack
    Naveen Kumar Vadlamudi and N. Ravi Shankar
    In Progress in Computing, Analytics and Networking: Proceedings of ICCAN 2019, 2020
    Abs DOI Bib

    A chosen-ciphertext attack on a published color-image cipher. The scheme uses a chaotic map to build key material, and the attack shows that the construction collapses to an affine Hill cipher.
