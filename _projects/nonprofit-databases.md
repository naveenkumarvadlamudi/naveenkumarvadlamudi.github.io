With Sidney J. Shapiro, Vivian Oystrick, and Alison Liu, I co-authored a *Canadian Journal of Program Evaluation* article on the point at which a non-profit should move program-evaluation data from spreadsheets into a database.

The paper walks through SQL, NoSQL, and desktop options such as Microsoft Access, and through the constraints that decide the choice: thin infrastructure, little full-time IT staff, and the need to train the people who will live with the system. I came to the paper from the data-engineering side, including a Mitacs data-management project at Lethbridge in early 2024.
