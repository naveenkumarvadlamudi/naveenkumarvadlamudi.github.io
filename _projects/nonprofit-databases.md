With Sidney J. Shapiro, Vivian Oystrick, and Alison Liu, I co-authored a *Canadian Journal of Program Evaluation* article on the point at which a non-profit should move program-evaluation data from spreadsheets into a database.

The paper walks through SQL, NoSQL, and desktop options such as Microsoft Access, and through the constraints that decide the choice: thin infrastructure, little full-time IT staff, and the need to train the people who will live with the system. I came to the paper from the data-engineering side. In the same Mitacs term, January 4 to April 25, 2024, I designed data management for a beef feedlot company: four sources into one analytics platform, and a blueprint that cut projected processing costs by about 25 percent. That design is separate from this article.
