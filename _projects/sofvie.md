Since 2024 I have worked on the database behind Sofvie, a safety platform for mining operations. The work covers stored procedures, production promotions and backups, REST APIs, and the reports the application shows to operators and managers.

As a student database administrator I also built reporting procedures, chose indexes for the product schema, and automated tasks that stream data into the system.
