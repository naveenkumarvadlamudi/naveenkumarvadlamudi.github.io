In summer 2023 I was a technical specialist for University of Lethbridge Facilities. I designed a path from source systems into SQL Server. Airflow orchestrated ingestion, dbt and stored procedures transformed the data, and a custom Docker image carried the deployment.

I wrote model tests and user documentation, trained the business owners, and promoted the jobs through QA into production. A workflow that had taken two or three days by hand finishes in under ten minutes.
