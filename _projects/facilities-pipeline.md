From May 1 to September 5, 2023 I was a technical specialist (data engineer) at the University of Lethbridge. I designed the data architecture and automated SQL Server ingestion for HR data. Airflow DAGs and dbt models replaced a manual process.

The existing process improved by about 100 times. Full automation saved about 10 or more hours a week and removed payroll discrepancies that came from manual entry.
