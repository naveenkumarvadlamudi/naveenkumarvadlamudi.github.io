At ValueMomentum I worked on a Snowflake modernization for a U.S. insurance carrier. Source data moved from on-premises systems through Amazon S3 into Snowflake landing and curated layers. I owned one subject area, including the historic and incremental fact jobs that went to production, SCD Type 2 audit packages in Matillion, and Snowsight reports for daily runtime and volume.

Parallel execution in Matillion brought that subject area from about 5.5–6.5 hours down to about 3–3.5 hours, and then to about 1.5–2.5 hours. Snowflake credit consumption fell to about 60 percent of the previous level, a saving of roughly $5,000–$6,000 a month.
