At ValueMomentum I was a cloud data engineer in Hyderabad from December 2, 2020 to August 29, 2022. I built Matillion ETL pipelines and a Snowflake cloud data warehouse for a U.S. insurance client, using AWS S3, EC2, and EMR, with Python and SQL.

The pipelines processed more than 10 million records and were delivered two weeks ahead of schedule. Pipeline runtime was optimized by 60 percent, and analytical query runtimes fell by 60 percent. Cloud cost work, including right-sizing, scheduling, and storage tiering, saved about $12,000 a month, about 35 percent.
